What we do

A shift in awareness begins
with understanding, and takes
hold through practice.

78.3% of working-age Poles report at least one of fourteen burnout symptoms, according to UCE RESEARCH. The most common is prolonged fatigue that doesn't go away with rest. It's reported by 43.4% of respondents.

On top of that, there's a new source of tension. According to a 2025 NASK report, around 5.08 million jobs in Poland — 30.3% of all positions — are vulnerable to changes driven by artificial intelligence (AI). Among office workers, that figure reaches 71.2%.

Behind these numbers are everyday experiences: evenings spent thinking about work, mornings without energy, trouble sleeping and digesting, and the sense that life is happening somewhere just out of reach.

The Conscious Human Foundation runs workshops for people who want to deal with the stress of work, personal life, and a world that's changing fast in the age of AI.

How we work

Methods that actually work.

We name what's invisible

The problem you arrive with is rarely the real one. We start by understanding it, not by fixing it.

We work individually and in small groups

It's easier to change habits together than when we try to change our behaviour alone.

We give tools, not prescriptions

We don't have five rules for a happy life. We have tools that work, if you put them into practice with our help.
